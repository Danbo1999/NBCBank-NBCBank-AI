Detailed Analysis

1. Top Candidate Profile

The top-ranked candidate, trans-resveratrol (FC03023), demonstrates the highest homology with Ursolic Acid, sharing 64 targets in total. Notably, 61 of these are core targets and 32 are enriched targets, indicating a very strong overlap in primary and high-confidence biological pathways. This compound is supported by 268 individual evidence records and is found in 2 related natural plants.

2. Ranking and Evidence Strength

Candidates are ranked using a weighted scoring system that prioritizes overlap with the template's core targets (fccore), followed by enriched targets (fcRICH/fcRTAR), and finally the total number of shared targets. This ensures that candidates with the most significant and well-supported target overlaps are presented first. The support_record_count provides additional context on the volume of underlying evidence for each candidate.

The overlapping target lists for the top candidates are dominated by genes involved in critical cellular processes:

  • Apoptosis & Cell Survival: BAX, BCL2, BCL2L1, CASP3, CASP8, CASP9.

  • Metabolic Regulation: EGFR, FASN, MTOR.

This pattern suggests the homologous development strategy is effectively identifying compounds with potential roles in anti-inflammatory, anti-proliferative, and pro-apoptotic activities, which are commonly associated with Ursolic Acid.
