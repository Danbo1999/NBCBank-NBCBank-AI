Among these, 83 components are classified as active (target-linked) components, while the remaining 84 components are classified as non-target components. The analysis provides a comprehensive inventory for further pharmacological or nutritional research.

Detailed Analysis

1. Component Overview and Classification

The analysis successfully identified 167 unique chemical constituents within Huang Qi. The components are systematically categorized into two primary groups based on their known biological target associations in the database:

  • Target-linked Components (83 items): These are compounds with documented interactions with specific molecular targets (e.g., proteins, receptors). This category includes flavonoids (e.g., quercetin, kaempferol, formononetin), sterols (e.g., β-sitosterol), fatty acids (e.g., palmitic acid, linolenic acid), vitamins (e.g., nicotinic acid), and various other bioactive molecules like choline and sucrose.
  • Non-target Components (84 items): These are compounds currently not linked to specific molecular targets within the database. This group is rich in specialized Huang Qi metabolites, notably a series of astragalosides (I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VII, VIII) and their derivatives (e.g., acetylastragaloside), which are key marker compounds for Astragalus species. Other notable non-target components include cycloastragenol, various isoflavonoid glycosides, and several alkaloids and saponins.

2. Key Bioactive Compounds

The target-linked component list highlights several compounds with established pharmacological significance:

  • Quercetin & Rutin: Potent flavonoids with known antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and cardiovascular protective effects.
  • β-Sitosterol: A phytosterol with cholesterol-lowering and anti-inflammatory properties.
  • Formononetin & Calycosin: Major isoflavones in Huang Qi, associated with estrogenic, antioxidant, and cardioprotective activities.
  • Kaempferol & Isorhamnetin: Other flavonoids contributing to the overall antioxidant capacity.
  • Choline: An essential nutrient important for liver function, brain development, and neurotransmission.

Conclusions and Recommendations

  1. Comprehensive Phytochemical Profile: Huang Qi contains a complex mixture of 167 identified compounds, spanning flavonoids, saponins (astragalosides), sterols, fatty acids, and alkaloids. This diversity underpins its wide range of purported health benefits in traditional and modern applications.
  2. Research Focus Areas:
    • Target Deconvolution: The 84 non-target components, particularly the astragalosides, represent a significant opportunity for future research to elucidate their molecular mechanisms of action and identify potential protein targets.
    • Synergistic Effects: The activity of Huang Qi is likely due to the combined effects of multiple bioactive compounds (e.g., quercetin, formononetin, astragaloside IV). Research should investigate potential synergistic or additive interactions among these components.
  3. Quality Control & Standardization: The identified astragalosides (I-IV) and flavonoids (e.g., calycosin, formononetin) are critical markers for the quality control and standardization of Huang Qi extracts in product development.
  4. Database Enhancement: The analysis successfully mapped user query "Huang Qi" to the standardized plant name "黄芪". Future work could involve validating and expanding the target associations for the currently listed "non-target" components to enrich the mechanistic understanding of this important medicinal plant.
