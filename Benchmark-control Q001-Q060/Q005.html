Detailed Analysis

The query successfully identified DGAT1 as a standardized target and retrieved 38 associated trait records. The data reveals a strong and specific functional profile for this gene.

1. Trait Category Distribution:
The associated traits are organized into several primary categories (Trait_namec1), with the vast majority falling under "乳产量" (Milk Production) and its related sub-categories. A smaller number of traits are linked to "繁殖性能" (Reproductive Performance) and "综合性能" (Overall Performance).

2. Core Functional Focus - Milk Traits:
DGAT1 is overwhelmingly associated with milk-related traits, which can be further broken down:

  • Yield Traits: Milk yield, fat yield, protein yield, lactose yield, and energy yield.
  • Composition Traits: Milk fat percentage, protein percentage, lactose content, and fat-to-protein ratio.
  • Milk Health & Quality: Somatic cell count (an indicator of udder health).
  • Detailed Milk Components: A wide array of specific milk fatty acid contents (e.g., palmitic acid, oleic acid, conjugated linoleic acid), mineral contents (calcium, phosphorus, zinc), and protein composition (casein percentages).

3. Non-Milk Traits:
Beyond milk production, DGAT1 shows association with:

  • Reproductive Performance: Calving to conception interval and inseminations per conception.
  • Overall Performance: Length of productive life.

4. Biological Interpretation:
DGAT1 encodes a key enzyme (diacylglycerol O-acyltransferase 1) involved in the final step of triglyceride synthesis. Its strong association with milk fat content and yield is consistent with its biological role in lipid metabolism within the mammary gland. The associations with milk protein, lactose, and overall yield suggest pleiotropic effects or tight metabolic coordination. Links to reproductive and longevity traits may indicate broader metabolic or energy balance influences.

Conclusion and Recommendations

Conclusion:
DGAT1 is a well-characterized gene in Bos taurus with extensive and strong genetic associations, primarily influencing milk production traits, especially milk fat synthesis and composition. It also has secondary associations with reproductive efficiency and longevity.

Recommendations:

  1. Breeding Program Focus: DGAT1 should be considered a high-priority candidate gene for marker-assisted selection in dairy cattle breeding programs aimed at improving milk fat and protein yield.
  2. Functional Studies: The pleiotropic associations with various milk components warrant further investigation into DGAT1's regulatory network to understand its broader impact on milk synthesis.
  3. Trait Prioritization: When evaluating DGAT1 in genetic studies, researchers should prioritize the specific trait IDs listed (e.g., PP01010164 for milk fat yield) to ensure consistency and comparability across datasets.
  4. Cross-Trait Consideration: Breeders should be aware of the potential correlated responses in reproductive traits and productive life when selecting for DGAT1 variants associated with high milk yield.
