3. Data Insights:

  • The presence of some compounds with high trait counts but lower gene counts (e.g., Capsaicin) suggests they may influence inflammation through a more focused set of key regulatory genes that are connected to many traits.
  • The results provide a prioritized list for further investigation, from high-confidence, multi-target candidates to more specific ones.
