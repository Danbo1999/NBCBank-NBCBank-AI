  1. Primary Recommendation: Trans-Resveratrol (FC03023) is the top novel candidate for homology development based on Quercetin. Its exceptionally high core target overlap (95 genes) suggests a very similar mechanism of action, particularly in anti-inflammatory and cell-signaling pathways.
  2. Secondary Candidates: Galloyl-l-Epigallocatechol (FC00035) and Curcumin (FC02724) are also highly recommended. Their strong target profiles, combined with Curcumin's extensive existing research base, make them excellent candidates for further validation.

  4. Source Consideration: While Quercetin is sourced from 49 plants, the top novel candidates are associated with fewer natural sources (1-5 plants). This may influence sourcing strategy but also suggests more specific bioactive origins.
